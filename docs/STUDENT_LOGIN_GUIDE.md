# Student Login Guide

## How to Login as a Student

### Option 1: Register a New Student Account

1. **Navigate to Registration Page**
   - Go to `/auth/register` or click "Get Started" / "Sign Up" on the home page

2. **Fill in Your Information**
   - **Step 1**: Enter your first name, last name, and email
   - Click "Continue"

3. **Select Student Role**
   - **Step 2**: Select "Student" role from the role options
   - Fill in institution (optional) and department (optional)
   - Create a password (must be at least 8 characters with uppercase, number, and special character)
   - Confirm your password

4. **Complete Registration**
   - Click "Create Account"
   - You'll be redirected to the login page

5. **Login**
   - Go to `/auth/login`
   - Enter your email and password
   - Click "Sign In"
   - You'll be redirected to the Student Dashboard (`/student`)

### Option 2: Use Existing Student Account

If you already have a student account:

1. Navigate to `/auth/login`
2. Enter your email address
3. Enter your password
4. Click "Sign In"
5. You'll be redirected to your Student Dashboard

### Student Dashboard Features

Once logged in as a student, you can:
- View your grades and assignments (`/student`)
- See your performance statistics
- View rubric breakdowns
- Track your progress over time
- Export your grades

### Troubleshooting

**Can't login?**
- Make sure you registered with the "Student" role
- Check that your email and password are correct
- Ensure your account is active (contact admin if needed)

**Forgot password?**
- Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page
- Enter your email to receive a password reset link

**Need to change role?**
- Contact your institution administrator
- Or register a new account with the correct role
